Use your saved quotes in Claude

Add Quotify as a custom connector, and Claude can look up the lines you saved while you draft, research or think through a book.

Claude is good at writing and thinking alongside you. With Quotify connected, it can work from the passages you chose to keep, instead of paraphrasing an author from memory. Quotify connects as a custom connector, which is Claude's name for an MCP server.

Where you connect it in Claude

You add Quotify once, under Customize › Connectors, and it works in both Claude on the web and the Claude desktop app, which share connectors. After you sign in, turn Quotify on for a chat from the + menu under Connectors.

Custom connectors work on Claude's Free plan (one custom connector), Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise. On Team and Enterprise, an Owner adds Quotify for the organization first, then each person connects and signs in with their own Apple account, so everyone sees only their own library.

The live setup steps (choose the Claude tab) walk through every screen. If you also use Claude Code, it can use the connectors from your Claude account when you sign in with the same account; the Claude Code tab covers the terminal setup.

Before you connect

Turn on iCloud sync in Quotify and let your library finish syncing. During setup you sign in with the Apple account that library uses. Saving new quotes is a separate permission you can leave off.

What to ask Claude

  • “Find what I saved about courage, with authors.” A good first test, using a topic you know is in your library.
  • “I'm writing an essay on rest and ambition. Pull three quotes from my library that fit, and tell me where each might go in the argument.”
  • “What have I saved from Seneca? Group the quotes by theme and quote each one exactly.”
  • “Here's my draft introduction. Is there a line in my saved quotes that would make a stronger opening?”
  • “Save this line to Quotify: ‘…’ by Mary Oliver.” Claude asks before saving, and you can check the text and author first.

For writing, ask Claude to keep each quotation word for word and to put its commentary in a separate sentence. If nothing matches, it should say so rather than supply a quote you never saved.

What the connection can and can't do

Claude can search and read your saved quotes, including their author, your custom fields and dates, and, if you allow it, save new ones. It can't edit or delete anything in your library. Your quotes stay in iCloud, and you can disconnect Claude at any time from Your connections.

If Claude can't find your library

Check that Quotify is turned on for the current chat, that you signed in with the Apple account your library syncs to, and that iCloud sync is on in the app. Then ask for a quote you know you saved. Never paste an Apple password or a token into a chat; the sign-in happens on Quotify's page.
