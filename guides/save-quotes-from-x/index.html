How to save a post from X as a quote

Send a post from X to Quotify, review the words and author, and keep it with the rest of your saved quotes.

A bookmark is useful when you want to return to a conversation on X. To keep the words themselves alongside your book passages and other quotes, share the post to Quotify.

Save the post's text

  1. Open a public post on X and use its share options.
  2. Open the iPhone share sheet and choose Quotify. Look in More if the app is not in your visible actions.
  3. Quotify uses the shared post's text as the quote. Review the draft and author before saving, and check that the full text arrived; very long posts can be shortened.
  4. Open Quotify to find the saved quote in your library.

Sharing behavior can vary by post and by the X app version. Check which text arrived: an original post, a quoted post and a thread are different things. Do not assume that sharing one post saves the entire conversation.

When the words are in an image

A text post and an image quote need different capture methods. If the words only appear in a picture, choose a screenshot in Quotify's Photo flow and review the extracted result. The Instagram guide covers a similar image-to-quote workflow.

Keep enough context to trust it later

Check attribution rather than automatically treating the sharing account as the author. Keep a useful source reference with the quote. This matters when a line is quoted again elsewhere or when a conversation changes after you saved it.

For a passage you found on a website, share it from Safari instead. A shared Safari page keeps its link and title, which makes it easier to revisit the source.

Bring a saved line back into view

Find it by its text or author, mark it as a Favorite, and use your own library on a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget. A saved line can stay useful even when it is no longer near the top of a social feed.
