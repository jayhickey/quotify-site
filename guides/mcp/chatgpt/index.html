Connect Quotify to ChatGPT

Find your saved quotes in ChatGPT, understand the connection permissions, and use the current Quotify setup guide.

Connect Quotify to ChatGPT to find and use quotes you have already saved. The connection uses MCP, a way for an AI app to request tools and information from a service you authorize.

Before you connect

Have Quotify set up with iCloud sync turned on and your quotes synchronized. In the connection flow, sign in with the Apple account associated with that library. The app itself has no account to create; connecting an AI app still requires authorization.

Connect and authorize

Choose the current ChatGPT guide on the connection page. Available connection features depend on the ChatGPT client and account; check that guide rather than assuming every mobile or desktop account has the same controls.

The live connection page is the source for setup steps. Use it for installation and sign-in instead of copying an old configuration from an article. Review the permissions and return to ChatGPT after authorizing.

Try one small request

Start with a line you know is in your library: “Find the quotes I saved about courage and include their authors.” Check the returned quotes against your saved words before relying on them in something you publish.

For a broader task, ask for a few saved passages relevant to a paragraph you are writing. Distinguish quotations from the AI's own explanation. If you want a new quote saved, ask explicitly and review the proposed text and attribution.

Ground a discussion in your own reading

Try a concrete request such as “Find three passages I saved about attention, with their authors.” Ask ChatGPT to use the saved text as the quotation and put its interpretation in a separate paragraph. This makes it easier to check where the quotation ends and the discussion begins.

If nothing matches, broaden the search to an author or a word you remember saving. A failed search is a reason to check the library, rather than ask the model to invent a missing quote.

What the connection can do

It can find and read saved quotes and, with your permission, save new ones. It cannot edit or delete quotes. An AI response is not proof that a change was saved; check the tool's result and your synchronized library.

If sign-in or the library does not appear

Return to the live guide and check the client you selected. Confirm that the Apple account is the one used for your Quotify library and that iCloud sync is enabled. Finish authorization in the browser, then return to the AI app and try a known saved quote.

Do not paste an Apple password, private token or session data into a chat to repair a connection. Use the supported sign-in flow. You can use different connection guides for other AI apps without following this client's settings.
