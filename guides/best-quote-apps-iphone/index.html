Best iPhone apps for saving quotes and widgets

Choose an iPhone quote app for your own saved words, Home Screen widgets and the way you capture inspiration.

For an iPhone quote app, decide whether you want a feed of new quotes or a place for words you chose yourself. If your goal is a personal library that also appears on your Home Screen, compare the source of the quotes as carefully as the widget design.

Quotify publishes this guide. Checked against each app's App Store listing and docs in November 2026; features change, so check before you choose.

Look beyond the Home Screen screenshot

Ask how the widget gets its content. Can you add the passage you just read? Can you choose a smaller collection for the days when a particular author matters? Will you remember which source the words came from?

Quotify shows quotes from your own saved library on the Home Screen and Lock Screen. You can choose everything, Favorites, a particular author or a field you have added in Rotation. You can also put your own photo behind the Home Screen widget.

Match capture to the way you use your iPhone

If you often photograph book pages or collect screenshots, Smart Photo to Quote can turn an image into a draft you review. Snap a book page or e-reader screen, check the quote and author, then save. Share-sheet capture handles lines from other apps, while Record covers words you hear.

If your highlights already live in an ebook service, an app that imports them may suit you better. Quotify builds a library one chosen passage at a time.

Keep it readable

A short passage works better in a compact widget than a whole paragraph. Start with a quote you know, add the widget, and check it at the text size and Home Screen appearance you actually use. Use a larger layout when the words need more space.
