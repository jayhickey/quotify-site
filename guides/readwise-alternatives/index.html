Readwise alternatives for a personal quote library

Compare importing a highlights archive with collecting selected quotes from photos, share sheets and voice.

Choose a Readwise alternative by the job you want to preserve. Readwise is built around importing reading highlights. Quotify is a personal quote library you build from photos, share sheets and Record, then revisit through widgets and reminders.

Quotify publishes this guide. Checked against each app's App Store listing and docs in November 2026; features change, so check before you choose.

If Kindle synchronization is the requirement

Readwise's Kindle documentation covers its synchronization and clippings-file workflows. If that sync is what you rely on, compare sources before you switch.

Quotify doesn't import My Clippings.txt or sync a Kindle account. You can snap an e-reader screen or pick a screenshot from a reading app to keep one passage, but that won't bring over the highlights you already have.

If the goal is to keep a few meaningful lines close

Quotify can fit a smaller, deliberately chosen library that mixes sources. Snap a book page or e-reader screen, share a passage from another app, or use Record for words you hear. Review the author and source context, then save the quote.

A Home Screen or Lock Screen widget brings saved words back into your day. Favorites and Rotation help you decide which part of the library to see. This workflow is useful when capturing and seeing the line again matters more than importing everything you have ever highlighted.

Check the exit path too

Before you move a library, keep a backup and check what the new app can import, because export formats differ. Quotify can import and export CSV files and back up your library.

Once your quotes are in Quotify, you can also bring them into Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor. That works with the quotes you've saved; it doesn't import ebook highlights.
