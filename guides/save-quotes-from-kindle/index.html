How to save quotes from Kindle

Turn a photo of your Kindle screen or a screenshot from the Kindle app into a quote you can keep and revisit.

You can keep an individual Kindle passage in Quotify by photographing the e-reader screen or choosing a screenshot from the Kindle app. Smart Photo to Quote finds the words and author for you to review.

Quotify does not import My Clippings.txt or sync your Kindle account. If your goal is to bring across hundreds of existing highlights automatically, choose a highlights-import workflow instead.

From a Kindle e-reader

  1. Open the passage on your Kindle. Show the whole line you want to keep and increase the text size if it is difficult to read.
  2. In Quotify, open Photo and snap the e-reader screen. Keep the camera square to the screen and avoid glare.
  3. Review the extracted quote and author. Add or correct the author when the visible page does not identify them.
  4. Save the quote, keeping the book title and any location information you need in your own fields.

From the Kindle app on iPhone

Take a screenshot with the passage visible, then choose that image in Quotify's Photo flow. Check the saved words against the original before you finish. You are capturing the visible passage, not connecting Quotify to Amazon or importing a Kindle library.

What about highlights and notebook exports?

Highlighting inside Kindle remains useful when you want to keep your notes attached to the book. Import-focused services offer a different route for moving a collection of those highlights. For example, Readwise documents both Kindle synchronization and My Clippings imports.

Those files are not a Quotify input. Photographing the screen is the Quotify path for a few lines you want to keep close; a dedicated importer is a better fit for a large archive. The same photo method works when reading on another e-reader, including Kobo.

See the line again tomorrow

Once saved, a Kindle passage behaves like any other quote in your library. Find it by text or author, mark it as a Favorite, and show it on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Choose a set of saved quotes in Rotation when you want your widgets and reminders to focus on a particular author or field.
