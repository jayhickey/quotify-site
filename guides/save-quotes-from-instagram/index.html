How to save a quote from Instagram

Keep the words from an Instagram image in your own quote library instead of losing them among saved posts.

Saving an Instagram post keeps a place to return to inside Instagram. When it is the words in an image that you want to remember, share the post to Quotify and review the quote it finds.

Share the post into Quotify

  1. Open the Instagram post that contains the quote.
  2. Use the post's share options to open the iPhone share sheet, then choose Quotify. If it is not visible, look in More and add Quotify to your share actions.
  3. Quotify reads the quote from the post's image. Review the text and author before you save.
  4. Return to Quotify and check the saved quote in your library.

Post access and Instagram's sharing options affect what is available to another app. Do not assume every private post, carousel, Story or video can be read from a shared link.

When sharing does not bring across the image

If you can view the quote but the shared post is not available, choose a screenshot you have saved in Quotify's Photo flow. Review its text carefully, especially when a decorative font or a busy background makes a word ambiguous.

Keep the original author when they are identified. The account that posted an image is not always the author of the words. Add source context that will help you find the original again.

Make the words part of your own library

Search for the quote by its words or author, mark it as a Favorite, or show it with your other saved quotes on a widget. This makes a line useful outside the place you first found it.

When sharing a quote of your own, Quotify's Share Quote sheet offers Story, Square and Wide images. Keep attribution with the words and use a format suited to where you plan to share.
