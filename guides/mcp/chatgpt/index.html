Bring your saved quotes into ChatGPT

Connect Quotify to ChatGPT, then talk through an idea with the passages you saved on the table, not a half-remembered version of them.

ChatGPT is a good partner for talking an idea through. Connected to Quotify, it can bring the quotes you saved into that conversation: what you highlighted from a book last year, or every line you kept from one author. Quotify connects as a custom MCP server, which ChatGPT adds as a plugin.

Which ChatGPT plans can connect

On the web, adding Quotify works on ChatGPT Business, Enterprise and Edu. On Business, an admin or owner adds it for the workspace. ChatGPT Pro can connect for reading only, so ChatGPT can find your quotes but not save new ones. The connection isn't available in the ChatGPT mobile app.

Where you connect it in ChatGPT

On the web, you add Quotify from Plugins as a custom MCP server with OAuth sign-in, then mention it in a chat by typing @ and choosing Quotify. The ChatGPT desktop app has its own MCP servers setting and shares its setup with Codex.

Follow the live setup steps (choose the ChatGPT tab) for the exact screens on the web and in the desktop app. They're kept up to date as ChatGPT changes.

Before you connect

Turn on iCloud sync in Quotify and let your library finish syncing. During setup you sign in with the Apple account that library uses.

What to ask ChatGPT

  • “@Quotify Find three passages I saved about attention, with authors.” A quick test that it can reach your library.
  • “Using only quotes from my library, what do the authors I've saved disagree about when it comes to work?”
  • “I saved a few lines from Meditations. Quote them, then help me work out which idea I keep coming back to.”
  • “I'm leading a book club discussion on Walden. Pull what I saved from it and turn each into a discussion question.”
  • “Which of my saved quotes would answer this question a friend asked me: ‘How do you stay patient?’”

Ask ChatGPT to quote your saved text exactly and keep its own interpretation in a separate paragraph. If a search comes back empty, try an author or a word you remember instead of letting it fill the gap with a quote you never saved.

What the connection can and can't do

ChatGPT can search and read your saved quotes and, on plans that allow it and with your permission, save new ones. It can't edit or delete anything in your library. Your quotes stay in iCloud, and you can disconnect at any time from Your connections.

If ChatGPT can't find your library

Make sure you picked Quotify with @ in the chat, that you signed in with the Apple account your library syncs to, and that iCloud sync is on in the app. Never paste an Apple password or a token into a chat; sign in through the OAuth flow.
