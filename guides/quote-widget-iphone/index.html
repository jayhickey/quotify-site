Put your own quotes on an iPhone widget

Add your saved quotes to the Home Screen or Lock Screen, and choose which words you see each day.

A quote widget can show a daily feed, or it can bring back a line you chose yourself. Quotify widgets use your saved library: a sentence from a book, something a friend said, or a passage you want to remember.

Add a Home Screen quote widget

  1. Save at least one quote in Quotify and open the app.
  2. Touch and hold an empty area on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  3. Tap Edit, then Add Widget. Find Quotify, choose a size, and add the widget.
  4. Move it where you want it and finish editing.

Apple's widget instructions cover the current Home Screen and Lock Screen controls. Button placement can vary with your iOS version.

Add a Lock Screen quote widget

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, choose Customize, then select the Lock Screen. Open the widget area, choose Quotify and add a widget, then finish customization. If the area is full, remove another widget to make space.

Choose a short saved passage for a small widget. Longer passages may need more room than a compact Lock Screen layout can provide.

Choose which quotes appear

Use Rotation in Quotify to show everything, Favorites, a single author or a field you have added. A Favorites rotation is useful for a small set of lines you want to keep returning to. An author or book field can keep the widget close to what you are reading now.

You can put your own photo behind a Home Screen widget. That photo-background option is for the Home Screen; it does not put a photo behind a Lock Screen widget.

Choose a personal library or a quote feed

A feed widget is useful when you want someone else to choose the day's quote. A personal-library widget is useful when you already know the words you want to remember.

If Quotify isn't in the widget picker yet, open the app once, then try again. Save a few quotes first so the widget has something to show.
